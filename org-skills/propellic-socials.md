Write a social media post for Propellic about: $ARGUMENTS

## Structure

1. **Hook** — Attention-grabbing opening line (question, stat, or bold claim)
2. **Key message** — The core insight or value (2-3 sentences max)
3. **Call to action** — Clear next step for the reader

## Propellic social voice

- **Conversational and confident** — we're industry experts sharing real insights
- **Travel and hospitality focused** — speak to hoteliers, resorts, DMOs, and travel brands
- **Data-informed** — reference real trends, metrics, or case studies when relevant
- **Engaging, not salesy** — educate and inspire, don't hard-sell
- **Accessible** — explain SEO and digital marketing concepts in plain language

## Platform guidelines

**LinkedIn:**
- Professional tone, thought leadership angle
- 150-300 words, use line breaks for readability
- 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end
- Tag relevant companies/people when appropriate

**X (Twitter):**
- Concise and punchy, under 280 characters
- 1-2 hashtags max
- Thread format for deeper topics

**Instagram:**
- Visual-first — describe the ideal image/graphic to pair with
- Engaging caption, 50-150 words
- 5-10 hashtags in a comment block

Specify which platform(s) the post is for, or generate versions for all three.
