Write a professional Propellic client email about: $ARGUMENTS

## Structure

1. **Greeting** — Warm, first-name basis when possible
2. **Context** — Brief reminder of where things stand (1-2 sentences)
3. **Main message** — The core update, recommendation, or ask
4. **Next steps** — Clear, numbered action items with owners and dates
5. **Sign-off** — Warm close with availability to discuss

## Propellic email voice

- **Professional yet approachable** — we're trusted advisors, not vendors
- **Lead with value** — open with what matters to the client, not internal process
- **Be specific** — use real numbers, dates, and deliverable names
- **Keep it scannable** — short paragraphs, bullet points for lists
- **Confident but not pushy** — recommend clearly, don't hedge excessively
- **Travel/hospitality context** — Propellic specializes in SEO and digital marketing for the travel and hospitality industry

## Formatting

- Subject line: Clear and action-oriented
- Length: 150-300 words
- No jargon without context — if using SEO terms, briefly explain their impact
- End with a specific next step, not an open-ended "let me know"
